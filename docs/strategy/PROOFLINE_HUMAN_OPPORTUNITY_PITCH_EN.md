# Proofline — Opportunity Before Automation

Status: hackathon pitch draft, not a claim of commercial launch or PayPal affiliation.

## One-sentence message

AI should not only automate work. It should help people who have not yet built a reputation gain access to their first opportunities.

## The problem

A capable newcomer may have relevant skills but few ratings, references, or completed contracts. My own attempts to enter freelance marketplaces motivated this concept. This is personal experience, not proof that all existing platforms systematically exclude newcomers.

## The product

Proofline proposes one global freelance marketplace with two service categories:

- **AI Work:** agents, automation, chatbots, data analysis and development.
- **Global Services:** design, translation, marketing, writing and other eligible remote services.

A customer describes the task; AI helps interpret requirements and surface relevant skills; a human chooses a provider, reviews the quote, and explicitly approves the amount and terms. The same quote, contract, delivery, and payment-evidence flow serves both categories.

## Why PayPal

Cross-border services need a payment path. Historical owner-observed PayPal Sandbox Orders evidence illustrates a possible payment integration, not a live checkout, escrow product, approved platform partnership, or current fee-sharing agreement. A PayPal-operated or partner marketplace and lower-cost fee model remain proposals requiring legal, technical and commercial validation.

## What the prototype demonstrates

A local, synthetic journey with fictional providers, skill matching, versioned quotes, human approval, contract milestones, reviewer decisions, SQLite records and explicit payment boundaries. Separately labeled historical owner-observed Gemini and PayPal Sandbox evidence must not be presented as calls made by the demo browser.

## Three-minute judging story

1. **0:00–0:25 — Why:** New freelancers need a path to their first opportunity. The founder encountered the friction personally.
2. **0:25–0:55 — Who:** Show AI Work and Global Services as two categories of one marketplace, not two products.
3. **0:55–1:35 — How:** Enter a fictional customer request, inspect skills and gaps, create a quote and approve exact terms.
4. **1:35–2:05 — Trust:** Show contract, delivery review, audit record, and human authority over any payment.
5. **2:05–2:35 — Proof:** Distinguish synthetic browser execution from owner-observed historical Gemini and PayPal Sandbox evidence.
6. **2:35–2:55 — Future:** Propose opt-in real sellers, verified identity, disputes and legally approved cross-border payment integration; do not claim these exist today.

## Closing line

**Technology is the evidence. The marketplace is the mechanism. More opportunity for people is the goal.**

## Claims discipline

Do not claim actual sellers, production transactions, escrow, PayPal ownership, revenue, fee discounts, AI-only job focus, live Gemini calls in the browser, or confirmed commercial market impact. Any public release, video upload, Devpost submission, or new provider payment request requires explicit owner approval.
