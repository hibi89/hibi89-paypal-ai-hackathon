**Owner decision:** WORKNOVA is the approved product and film brand for the PayPal AI Hackathon, replacing Proofline in future creative output. It is a hackathon presentation name; trademark, company-name and domain clearance have not been performed.

## Brand definition
- Name: WORKNOVA (Work + Nova)
- Primary tagline: The Next Generation of Work.
- Supporting line: Where People and AI Work as One.
- Category: Global AI Service Commerce
- Promise: a global marketplace where people discover services and AI agents assist with matching, delegation, negotiation and delivery while humans retain approval authority.
- Positioning: not merely a Fiverr/크몽 clone. Part 1 is the service marketplace and freelance acquisition flow; Part 2 adds collaborative AI-agent workflows around the work and transaction.

## Integrated product: Part 1 + Part 2
### Part 1 — AI freelance/service marketplace
- Two service categories: AI Work and Global Services.
- Buyer discovers a service, explores specialists, requests a quote, reviews terms and explicitly approves.
- PayPal Sandbox payment evidence is presented truthfully; no live/Production payment claim.
- Human approval is the control point before any payment authority.

### Part 2 — AI agent work ecosystem
Five capability demonstrations:
1. Delegate & Consent — scoped task delegation and human consent.
2. A2A Negotiation — buyer/seller agents negotiate fictional terms.
3. Gift Finder — constrained recommendation from available local/sample catalog data.
4. Churn Saver — retention proposal.
5. Invoice & Dispute Copilot — invoice and dispute assistance.

### Integrated story
Discover → Match → Delegate / Negotiate → Human Approves → PayPal Flow → Evidence / Delivery.
Part 1 and Part 2 are one coherent product story. Current reviewer/browser flows are offline deterministic/synthetic unless a screen explicitly shows separate owner-observed evidence.
