# WORKNOVA — 175s owner-approved-content candidate (NOT PUBLISHED)

**Title:** WORKNOVA — One Marketplace for Human Talent and the AI Agent Economy

**Tagline:** Make every legitimate skill discoverable. Let AI coordinate work, while people authorize payments.

## Inspiration
Millions of people have useful abilities that can become services but struggle to be discovered. WORKNOVA envisions a global marketplace where people can productize any legitimate talent—far beyond AI development, design, translation or marketing. It also explores a future where AI agents help people discover work, negotiate and coordinate contracts. This is an independent prototype and aspirational model, not an existing userbase, reduced PayPal processing fee or commercial PayPal partnership.

## What it does
**One marketplace, two discovery paths.** Our reproducible local browser currently demonstrates AI Work and Global Services, both using fictional specialist profiles. AI Work shows AI-related services. Global Services is designed to grow into *any lawful personal skill offered as a service*; today's design, translation and marketing profiles are **illustrative test cases, NOT the end of the proposed catalog**. Reviewers can explore matching, missing skills, versioned quotes, human amount approval, contract delivery/revision and reviewer acceptance. All buyer, seller and payment activities in the default browser are offline synthetic fixtures.

**Separate Agentic Innovation Lab** offers five working local, deterministic modules: A2A buyer/seller negotiation, Delegate & Consent, Gift Finder, Subscription Churn Saver and Invoice/Dispute Copilot. A separate offline commerce-control dashboard demonstrates a bounded $500 policy budget, two A2A negotiations, synthetic delivery proof and a **human-only settlement review** gate. Its $460 reservation is local policy state, **not money charged or transferred**.

WORKNOVA's long-term goal integrates AI agents as participants in the broader human-talent market, rather than a disconnected technical lab. **That unified live commerce platform is future work**, not a deployed marketplace or completed PayPal agent-purchase integration.

## PayPal and AI
Human approval must bind exact amount/currency, merchant and quote version before any intended Sandbox Orders operation; a separately approved buyer and independently authorized capture/GET are required. Revocable agent authority and fail-closed payment recovery are implemented in the code. Owner reported October 28, 2026 historical Gemini classification and a manually approved $1.50 PayPal Sandbox order/capture/GET. This is not a fresh independently reproduced same-quote Gemini↔OIDC↔PayPal transaction. New integrated security regressions use **injected fake provider responses**, and the local demo sends **zero** external payment/AI requests. The aspiration is to help shape the next era of global commerce with PayPal; no PayPal partnership or leadership ranking is claimed.

## How it was built
Node.js 22.5+ (JavaScript ES modules), SQLite, HTML/CSS, separate local browser control planes, PayPal Sandbox and Gemini operator-gated adapters, strict approval/version/idempotency boundaries, Chromium/Playwright and GitHub Actions. Security deliberately separates suggested or agreed actions from payment authority.

## Challenges
Carefully separating agent agreement from human money permission, especially under ambiguous checkout POST outcomes. We forbid automatic repeat POST charges and prefer GET-only reconciliation. Current demos disclose fictional data and do not expose private API keys, buyer identities or private receipts.

## What we learned
A model agreement, SQLite event, mock browser test, historical Sandbox report and provider-signed fresh transaction are different evidence classes. Human consent and accurate provenance labels make agentic commerce safer.
